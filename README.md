# Kant Perspective Skill

A thinking framework distilled from Immanuel Kant's three Critiques, designed as a cognitive operating system for analyzing epistemological, ethical, and aesthetic questions.

## What's in this skill

This skill captures HOW Kant thinks, not just WHAT he said. It includes:

- **6 Mental Models**: Transcendental idealism, categorical imperative, autonomy, limits of reason, end-in-itself, sublime and purposiveness
- **5 Decision Heuristics**: Universalization test, humanity respect test, duty vs. conformity, knowable vs. unknowable, motive purification
- **Expression DNA**: Analysis of Kant's systematic, architectural writing style
- **Agentic Protocol**: Question routing system for different types of problems
- **7 Example Dialogues**: Covering ethics, epistemology, practical decisions, personal development, and aesthetic experience

## Source texts

This skill is based on four primary works (Cambridge University Press editions):

- *Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals* (1785)
- *Critique of Pure Reason* (1781/1787)
- *Critique of Practical Reason* (1788)
- *Critique of Judgment* (1790)

Note: The source PDFs are not included due to copyright. The research files contain original analysis and commentary, not reproductions of the source texts.
